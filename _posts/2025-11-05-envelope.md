This tutorial is for anyone who wants to gift or use cute, tiny monochromatic patterned envelopes. The printable is free to use, whether you’re not in a creative mood or simply don’t know where to start.
There are many similar printables online, but since I only have a monochrome printer, I noticed there aren’t many options made for it. You can print this template on colored paper, as I did, or on plain white paper and decorate it however you like!

### What you need:

- A pair of scissors

- Some glue (I used Vinavil with a small brush)

### Steps:

1. Print [this file](/assets/images/Envelope_temp.PNG) (A4 size)

2. Cut along the border

3. Fold along the dashed lines as in the pictures below (I didn’t include them in the printable version, as they can be visible and not very pleasing)
{% include gallery id="gallery" caption="Steps 2. and 3." %}

4. Glue the sides together as shown

5. Showcase them to your friends!
